get in touch.

Still have a question? Message Mc Bobo ↗

How far in advance should I book?+

Popular dates can fill early, especially during wedding season. Get in touch as soon as you have a date in mind and we’ll check availability.

What types of events do you host?+

Weddings, corporate events, social celebrations, religious occasions, brand appearances and special events.

Do you host corporate events?+

Yes. Get in touch about conferences, launches, dinners and other corporate gatherings.

Do you travel outside Osogbo?+

Share your event location when you enquire so travel and availability can be discussed.

Can you coordinate the event programme?+

Event coordination, including programme flow, timing and execution support, is available to discuss.

How do I check your availability?+

Complete the enquiry form or send a WhatsApp message with your event type, date and location.

How do I book?+

Start with an enquiry. After confirming availability, you can discuss your requirements and agree the booking details directly.
